The two notices were compared and are byte-identical after CRLF-to-LF
normalization. No license wording was changed.

CPython's Windows installer copies the bundle's lib subtree. Tk's license
is within lib/tk8.6, while Tcl's notice is outside lib at the path above:
https://github.com/python/cpython/blob/v3.12.10/Tools/msi/tcltk/tcltk.wixproj

The collector may use this pinned notice only when the active interpreter's
"info patchlevel" is exactly 8.6.15 and no installed Tcl notice was found.
It verifies the LF-normalized SHA-256 before copying the notice. Other
versions, missing/corrupt notices, and missing Tk/PyInstaller notices remain
errors. There are no build-time or runtime license downloads.
